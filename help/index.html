What DETOXY Plus is

DETOXY works without paying: the home screen, search, hiding and renaming apps, a pause on up to 3 apps, focus sessions, Time in apps, emergency access and every child-safety feature are free. DETOXY Plus adds pauses on as many apps as you like, longer pauses when you come back quickly, daily limits, focus profiles with schedules and Do Not Disturb, Pause everything, your daily routine with reminders and the Quick Settings tile.

You can see everything Plus includes, the price and the trial in DETOXY: Settings → Subscription.

Price and free trial

  • DETOXY Plus is a monthly subscription sold through Google Play. The price Google Play shows you before you confirm is the price you pay.
  • New subscribers get a free trial. DETOXY shows how long it is and the date it ends before you start it.
  • Google Play asks for a payment method (UPI, card or another method Play offers) to start the trial. You are not charged during the trial.
  • Unless you cancel before the trial ends, Google Play charges the monthly price on the day it ends, and then every month until you cancel.
  • The free trial is for new subscribers only: one per Google account. Google Play decides who can get it.
  • DETOXY reminds you 30, 7, 3 and 1 days before the trial ends, and on the last day. You can turn these reminders off in Settings → Subscription.

How to cancel

  • In Google Play: open the Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DETOXY → Cancel subscription.
  • Or in DETOXY: Settings → Subscription → Manage or cancel in Google Play, which opens the same page.
  • Uninstalling DETOXY, or "Erase all DETOXY data", does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
  • After you cancel you keep DETOXY Plus until the end of the time you already have. If you cancel during the free trial you are never charged.
  • When DETOXY Plus ends, DETOXY keeps working on the free features and nothing you set up is deleted. If you subscribe again, everything comes back as it was.

Was I charged?

  • Google Play emails a receipt for every payment. It has an order number that starts with GPA.
  • To see every payment: Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.
  • During the free trial no payment is taken. Some banks show a small check of the payment method that is reversed; that is between Google and your bank.

Refunds

  • Payments are handled by Google Play, so refunds are made through Google Play. Start with Google: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history → the order → Request a refund, or search Google's help for "Request a refund on Google Play".
  • If Google can't help, write to us at the contact address above with the order number (GPA.…). Don't send anything else about your payment.
  • If a payment is refunded, DETOXY Plus ends for that payment.

A payment didn't go through

Google Play tries again for a few days and tells you how to fix the payment method. You keep DETOXY Plus during that time. To update the payment method: Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DETOXY → Update. If the payment still fails, DETOXY Plus pauses until it is fixed; nothing is deleted.

New phone, or DETOXY reinstalled

Sign in to the same Google account in the Play Store, open DETOXY → Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Your subscription is tied to that Google account, not to the phone. You don't need to pay again, and the free trial isn't offered a second time.

Safety

DETOXY never asks for your card number, UPI PIN, OTP or password, by email, phone or chat. Payments happen only inside Google Play. If anyone asks you for these in DETOXY's name, don't share them.
