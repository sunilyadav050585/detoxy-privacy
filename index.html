Summary

DETOXY does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, no account system, no advertising and no analytics.

What DETOXY stores on your phone

To work, DETOXY keeps the following only on your device, in its private app storage:

  • the apps you place on the home screen, and their order
  • the apps you choose to pause, the apps you mark as essential, the apps you hide from the app list, and any names you give apps
  • your settings (theme, text size, pause length, focus profiles, focus sessions and schedules, "Pause everything", daily time budgets)
  • simple counters: pauses started, closed without opening, opened after the pause, emergency opens, and how many times you chose each answer to "What did you open this for?". These are totals only, with no record of which app or when.
  • for each app that got a pause today: how many pauses it got today and when the last one started. DETOXY uses this to show "2 tries today" and to lengthen the pause if you come back within 5 minutes. It is replaced the next day and is never a history of your app use.
  • your answers to "How will you use DETOXY?": Personal or Student, the goals you picked, and for students an age group (13–17 or 18 and over). They change wording and suggestions only. No date of birth.
  • your timetable (DETOXY Daily), if you make one: each activity's name, kind, times, days and reminder choice, and which activities you marked Done. Done marks are deleted after 14 days.
  • your focus timer settings (lengths, rounds, profile, strict mode, sound and volume), the timer that is running, and, for each of the last 90 days, how many focus rounds you finished and their minutes. DETOXY uses this to show your streak and your Reports. Older days are deleted.
  • your blocking choices (blocking everywhere, Shorts, Reels, YouTube Study Mode), the channel names you add to your study list, and when you agreed to the Accessibility explanation.
  • your choice of Black & white or Colour.

If you turn on Time in apps (optional, off by default), DETOXY reads Android's record of which apps were on screen today, through Android's "Usage access" setting. It uses this for these things only: to show today's time next to app names, to apply any daily time budgets you set (once an app's budget is used up, opening it from DETOXY gets a longer pause), and to show your Reports and the optional Sunday weekly summary (time per app and how many times the phone was unlocked, for today and the last 7 days). It reads this on your phone when you open DETOXY, an app with a budget or the Reports page, doesn't save it, and can't send it anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Special app access → Usage access. Without it, everything else works and budgets simply don't apply.

DETOXY reads the list of apps installed on your phone that have a launcher icon, so it can show them as text. It uses this list only on your device and does not save or send it.

Blocking everywhere (optional, uses Android's Accessibility Service)

This is off by default. It only starts if you read DETOXY's explanation in Settings → Focus & study → Blocking everywhere, tap "Agree", and turn DETOXY on in Android's Accessibility settings. While it is on, DETOXY uses Android's Accessibility Service to:

  • see which app comes to the front, so a paused app opened from a notification, the recent apps screen or another home screen gets DETOXY's pause, and strict focus applies everywhere;
  • in YouTube and Instagram only, check whether the Shorts or Reels player is on screen (by the names of on-screen elements), and close it if you asked DETOXY to;
  • in YouTube Study Mode, read the channel name shown on a video page, and leave the video if that channel is not on your study list.

DETOXY reads this only at that moment, on your phone. It does not store it, log it or send it anywhere. It does not read what you type, your messages, passwords or other screen content, and it does not type, post or change anything for you; the only actions it takes are showing its own pause screen, pressing Back or Home, and, when Reels are blocked, switching Instagram to its own Home tab. It never blocks Android Settings and never stops you turning it off or uninstalling any app. Turn it off at any time in DETOXY (Turn off blocking everywhere) or in Android Settings → Accessibility.

DETOXY Plus (subscription)

DETOXY Plus is an optional subscription (a free trial, then a monthly price shown before you buy). Google Play handles the purchase: your payment method, card, UPI or bank details go to Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), never to DETOXY. On your phone, DETOXY asks the Google Play app whether your Google account has DETOXY Plus and keeps the answer in its private storage: the subscription's status, Google's purchase time, the trial's end date, whether it renews, and Google's purchase reference (a "token"). It uses this only to switch Plus features on or off and to remind you before a free trial ends (if notifications are allowed). It is not sent anywhere by DETOXY.

Google Play's billing component inside DETOXY includes a Google library that would send diagnostic information to Google; DETOXY removes its internet permission, so it can't send anything from DETOXY. Google Play itself (a separate app) handles your purchase under Google's terms.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you save a backup yourself. DETOXY does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. Its data is also excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Settings backup (optional). Settings → Backup → "Save to a file" writes a plain-text file with your home-screen apps, paused, essential and hidden apps, app names, settings, focus profiles and daily budgets, your timetable, your focus timer settings and your blocking choices with your study channel list. It does not include the counters, today's tries, Done marks, your focus history, your Accessibility agreement or your answers to "How will you use DETOXY?". You choose where the file goes using Android's file picker. If you choose a cloud service (for example Google Drive), that service stores the file under its own privacy policy. DETOXY never sends the file anywhere itself, and "Erase all DETOXY data" doesn't delete backup files you saved; delete them where you saved them.

Permissions

DETOXY does not request internet access. It asks for one permission while you use it: notifications, the first time you set a reminder on your timetable (Android 13 and later), so reminders, the focus timer's countdown and the optional weekly summary can appear. If you say no, everything else works without reminders. It also declares two optional special permissions, which do nothing unless you turn them on yourself in Android Settings after reading DETOXY's explanation:

  • Usage access, to show today's time in apps and apply daily limits.
  • Do Not Disturb access, only if you ask a focus profile to turn on Do Not Disturb. DETOXY uses it to switch its own "DETOXY focus" rule on and off; it does not read your notifications or change your other Do Not Disturb settings.
  • Accessibility, only for Blocking everywhere, as described above.

To start and end that rule on time, and to show timetable reminders on time, DETOXY may also set alarms ("Alarms & reminders") and is told when the phone restarts or its clock changes. These are not used for anything else. The focus timer uses the same alarms to end each round and break on time. If you choose a calm sound for focus, DETOXY plays it as a "media playback" foreground service (with a notification you can stop it from); the sound is generated on your phone, nothing is downloaded. So that swiping down on the home screen can show your notifications, DETOXY also holds Android's "expand status bar" permission, which is granted on install, shows no prompt, and only pulls down the notification shade; it cannot read notifications. It can be set as your home app through Android's standard "Home app" setting, which you can change at any time.

Deleting your data

Open DETOXY → Settings → "Erase all DETOXY data", or uninstall the app. Either removes everything DETOXY stored, including its copy of your subscription status (DETOXY asks Google Play again the next time it opens). Erasing or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Children

DETOXY is for ages 13 and up, is not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone. The Student setup offers only "13 to 17" and "18 or older" and asks younger children to talk to a parent or guardian.
